Cookie policy.

How Workbloom uses cookies and browser storage to provide the service and remember your choices.

Necessary cookies and optional statistics

We use necessary cookies and requested preferences to provide the service. The banner lets you accept, reject or customise first-party statistics, with accept and reject equally prominent. We do not use advertising cookies or external analytics. Before a choice or after refusal, only anonymous cookieless counts remain; GPC and Do Not Track signals are treated as refusal.

Workbloom cookies and storage

Cookies are small pieces of data sent to your browser; sessionStorage holds data for an individual tab session. Items in the Workbloom table below are first-party on our domain; Cloudflare Turnstile is described separately as a third-party security tool. Supabase names depend on the project reference. We do not use localStorage, IndexedDB or advertising-tracking widgets: the Turnstile widget is used only for anti-bot security on /accesso and /registrati. Fonts are local; blog images may come from Supabase Storage.

Cloudflare Turnstile security

Cloudflare Inc. provides Turnstile to protect sign-in and sign-up forms against automated abuse. Its script and widget are loaded from challenges.cloudflare.com only on /accesso and /registrati, when the service is configured. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and browser/device signals and may use its own cookies or tokens on the Cloudflare domain, not Workbloom profiling cookies. The challenge token is submitted with the form and verified by Supabase Auth.

Managing and deleting stored data

Open ‘Cookie preferences’ in the footer to change or withdraw consent at any time. The choice lasts 6 months (180 days); we ask again when the policy version changes. Refusal disables optional statistics and removes their cookies on the next request to the collection service. GPC and Do Not Track keep statistics off while the browser signal remains active.

You can change language and theme using the site controls. To delete cookies and storage, open your browser settings, find Privacy / Cookies and site data, select the Workbloom domain and clear its data; select Cloudflare separately and, for external checkout, Stripe domains. You can also block cookies in browser settings. To clear sessionStorage drafts, close the tab and, if your browser restores sessions, also clear site data.

Clearing data can sign you out, remove unsent drafts, reset preferences and prevent that browser from recovering guest chats. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in and chat. To erase server-held data or exercise your rights, contact the controller: clearing cookies is not an account-erasure request.
