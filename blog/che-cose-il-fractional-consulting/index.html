What is fractional consulting?

#FutureOfWork #Consulting #Workbloom

Workbloom
16 April 2026

It is a model in which senior professionals work on specific projects for several companies. They are not hired full-time. They step in when their expertise is needed.

For companies, this means:

  • access to high-level expertise
  • greater organisational flexibility
  • more affordable costs

For professionals, it means making an impact across more projects.

This model is growing rapidly in many markets.

More and more organisations are rethinking how they access strategic expertise.

Workbloom was created to meet this need: a platform that enables companies to bring in senior expertise on demand, when they need it.
