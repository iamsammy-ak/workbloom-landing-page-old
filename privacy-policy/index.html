Privacy Policy.

Information regarding personal data processing in this release of the platform.

Your data, the current service

This notice explains how the live Workbloom service processes personal data, the applicable legal bases, providers and your rights. Online payments are not yet open to customers; checkout is being tested internally. Contact privacy@workbloom.io for information or to exercise your rights. No unverified compliance certification is claimed.

1.Data Controller & Contact

Workbloom S.r.l., Innovative Startup, Via Sardegna 32, 20146 Milan, Italy. Tax Code and VAT registration number 13981770962. For privacy and data inquiries: privacy@workbloom.io or info@workbloom.io.

2.Scope of This Policy

This document covers the current Workbloom web application and backend infrastructure under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Regulation EU 2016/679) and applicable privacy laws. Statements, registrations, brokers, or retention metrics from older legacy systems are not imported into this platform.

3.Data Processed

Company accounts use verified work email addresses; Bloomers may use their own verified email. Company profiles contain business and representative details, with optional invoicing information visible only to the company Owner. Needs contain business problems, objectives and constraints. Private professional applications contain contact details, expertise and specializations, experience and project summaries, working preferences, and optional portfolio or professional-registration information.

Technical connection data may appear in system logs. Such logs are not described as fully anonymous or aggregated without specific technical auditing. Do not enter third-party personal data or proprietary secrets into briefs unless authorized to share them.

4.Confidential Access & Bloomer Privacy

Conversations and confirmed requirements are restricted to authorized members of the corresponding company workspace. Imported Bloomer records are private and there is no open specialist directory. Following essential follow-ups and verification of a complete company account, automatic anonymous matching displays only anonymous, limited candidate information until an authorized identity unlock succeeds.

Names and contact details are disclosed only within the authorized company after a successful token debit from an eligible verified live purchase or a grant by the Workbloom platform owner. Test payments cannot reveal real identities. Approval alone never publishes a profile.

A Bloomer can read and edit their own profile after verified-email ownership checks. Pending and rejected applications are excluded from matching. All approved profiles participate in matching using their available professional evidence, regardless of completeness, specialization depth, availability status, or confirmation date, while profile completeness and accuracy confirmations serve to improve matching quality rather than exclude candidates.

5.Drafts, Cookies & Communications

Unsent chat drafts stay in the tab’s sessionStorage. Sent visitor messages and draft needs are stored privately in Supabase. Each visitor conversation remains accessible for 24 hours after the visitor’s latest message; sending another message renews that period, while starting a new chat does not delete earlier conversations. Expired visitor records are removed by a scheduled cleanup every 15 minutes. Essential HttpOnly cookies identify the visitor’s chats and prevent other visitors from reading them. After verified signup or login, the visitor can save the reviewed conversation into their company account; that copied account history is not deleted by visitor-session expiry. Authenticated messages, confirmed need versions and in-app professional communications remain private under the account retention policy, whose final wording requires approval. Avoid shared workstations. Use Log out to end an authenticated session; closing a tab does not necessarily terminate it.

Approved Bloomer professional evidence is indexed privately in Supabase using multilingual embeddings generated on the application server, rather than an external embedding API. The search source selects professional fields, not identity or fiscal fields. Missing specializations or other incomplete details do not remove an approved profile from matching; the results disclose evidence gaps. Withdrawal of approval removes the profile’s search documents and vectors, while its private administrative record remains available to authorized staff.

The public contact form opens your local email client and does not submit its draft to our servers. In-app messages are stored by the platform. BlooMIA sends relevant conversation context, confirmed requirements and retrieved anonymous professional evidence to OpenAI (United States) and, only if OpenAI is unavailable, to OpenRouter and its selected free-model provider. Matching/assessment context excludes candidate names, contacts, employers, raw roles and biographies. OpenAI requests ask OpenAI not to store the completion; OpenRouter requests deny providers marked as collecting user data. Neither is a contractual zero-retention or EU-residency guarantee. Do not share unauthorized confidential or personal information. Stripe hosts payment checkout; Workbloom does not store card details or record calls. Online payments are not yet open to customers; only internal checkout tests are currently available to staff.

CV-assisted onboarding is optional. After your explicit permission, a PDF or UTF-8 text document is parsed in memory and its extracted text is sent to OpenAI (United States) or, only if OpenAI is unavailable, to OpenRouter and the selected free-model provider. Unlike anonymous matching, this text can include your name and any other personal information you leave in the document: remove unnecessary or sensitive details first. Workbloom does not persist the original file or extracted text. Suggested professional fields appear for your review and are saved only when you save or submit your profile. Enter everything manually instead if you do not want this AI processing. Provider retention and international-transfer terms still require final privacy review.

BlooMIA is an AI assistant, not a person, and can make mistakes or produce incomplete information. Messages are processed by AI providers via API. Matching supports human evaluation: the Workbloom team reviews professional profiles and suitability claims, and companies must review suggested matches before proceeding. Workbloom does not use conversations to train its own models.

6.Purposes & Legal Bases

We process data to provide guest chat, accounts and profiles, private conversations, matching and internal candidate review, and to secure the service. The table maps these purposes to the applicable GDPR legal bases. Providing the data needed for an account or requested service is necessary to deliver it; optional CV parsing can be declined without preventing manual profile completion.

No automatic marketing subscriptions, promotional campaigns, or bulk invites are sent to candidates purely by virtue of record importation. Inclusion in the candidate archive does not constitute consent for public web display.

7.Infrastructure, Hosting & Cookies

Database storage and authentication run on Supabase, hosted within the European Union (Ireland region). Providers process the data needed for their respective services; the list below identifies their roles and data categories. Database geography alone is not a guarantee that every processing operation stays within the EU.

Workbloom uses necessary technical cookies/storage and requested preferences, plus optional first-party statistics enabled only through the consent banner. Necessary cookies are workbloom-language and workbloom-theme (365 days), bloomia_guest_secret and bloomia_guest_active (24 hours), wb_consent (choice, policy version, date and identifier; six months/180 days), and Supabase authentication/helper cookies (configured maximum 400 days; authentication helpers are removed during cleanup and this is not the server session's authorization period). Optional statistics cookies are wb_vid (browser identifier, 13 months) and wb_sid (session identifier, 30 minutes of inactivity). Change the choice using Cookie preferences in the footer; GPC and Do Not Track are treated as refusal. Before consent or after refusal, only anonymous cookieless counting remains. With consent, statistics cover visits, interactions, sources and approximate location; signed-in sessions may be linked to the account. Statistics are retained for 26 months; evidence of the choice is retained for five years without IP addresses. Tab sessionStorage holds unsent chat drafts. No advertising/profiling cookies, external analytics or translator widgets are deployed. Fonts are served locally; blog images may be served through Supabase Storage. The Cookie policy at /cookie-policy (also linked in the footer) lists names, purposes, durations, deletion instructions and the separate Stripe-hosted checkout.

Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, the applicable safeguards are the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the receiving provider is certified and the transfer is covered, otherwise the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, with supplementary measures where required. You can request information about the applicable safeguards from privacy@workbloom.io.

  • Supabase Pte. Ltd. (Supabase): database, authentication and storage; account identifiers, emails, profiles, conversations, business needs and service records in the Ireland database region.
  • Vercel Inc.: application hosting, server functions and edge delivery; IP addresses, request headers and request/service logs, and application data processed to fulfil requests.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (Turnstile): anti-bot security on /accesso; IP address, browser/device signals and challenge tokens, with its own cookies/tokens on challenges.cloudflare.com.
  • Resend Inc.: transactional authentication emails from mail.workbloom.io; recipient email addresses, authentication-email content and delivery metadata.
  • Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft 365): company mailboxes; sender/recipient addresses, email content, attachments and correspondence metadata for support, privacy and partnership requests.
  • OpenAI Ireland Ltd., with OpenAI OpCo, LLC (United States): AI processing via API; relevant conversation context, requirements, anonymous professional evidence and, with consent, extracted CV text.
  • OpenRouter, Inc. and the selected model provider: fallback AI API routing and processing only if OpenAI is unavailable; the same relevant input categories, including extracted CV text when consented.
  • Stripe Payments Europe, Limited and Stripe, Inc.: hosted checkout and payment/fraud services; billing details, transaction identifiers and payment data submitted directly to Stripe. Customer online payments are not yet open; current checkout use is limited to internal staff tests. Stripe also acts as an independent controller for its own legal and fraud-prevention purposes.

8.Data Retention

Data retention is limited to specified service purposes and applicable legal requirements. Accounting and tax records are kept for 10 years under Article 2220 of the Italian Civil Code. Guest-chat expiry and cleanup are described in section 5. The remaining detailed retention schedule and operational disposal procedures require privacy approval; neither arbitrary fixed deadlines nor indefinite storage are promised.

There is no self-service data export or deletion portal. A staff-only export tool supports the handling of requests; it is not accessible to customers. For data subject inquiries, contact the controller, who will verify requests in accordance with statutory rights and compliance duties.

9.Security & Sessions

The database enforces Row Level Security and role-based access separation. Operations on specialist records strictly require an internal staff role verified server-side; client company credentials or client-declared roles cannot grant staff privileges.

The public service is served over HTTPS at workbloom.io, and connections to the Supabase backend use HTTPS. Application security headers include Content-Security-Policy with frame-ancestors 'none' and X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff. These safeguards complement access controls; they are not a guarantee of absolute security.

10.Data Subject Rights

To exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or objection under the GDPR, write to privacy@workbloom.io. The controller will verify requester identity and respond in accordance with statutory requirements without compromising third-party data.

Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Optional AI-assisted CV parsing is not required: you can enter your profile manually. Company workspace closure and personal-data erasure are separate requests; privacy@workbloom.io also handles export and erasure requests.

You retain the right to lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority. In Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).
