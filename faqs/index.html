For companies

Finding expertise, using Workbloom and understanding costs.

What can I do on Workbloom?

Start a conversation with BlooMIA without an account. To compare approved profiles, you need a verified, complete company account. Assess experience before unlocking; contact details follow once the Bloomer accepts your request.

What is Workbloom and how does it work?

Workbloom connects companies with independent consultants and managers, known as Bloomers. BlooMIA helps clarify your goals, context and required expertise. You compare relevant profiles, then agree any engagement directly with the professional.

Talk to BlooMIA
Who are the consultants on Workbloom?

Independent consultants and managers whose experience, projects and supporting information are reviewed by the team. This is not independent certification of their credentials. Profiles are not public: the name and full profile are shown after an authorised unlock, and contact details once the Bloomer accepts a contact request.

About the Bloomers
What expertise can I find on Workbloom?

Strategy, finance, technology, people management and other specialist expertise. Explore the areas and example business challenges.

Explore all expertise
How do I find the right consultant for my company?

Tell BlooMIA your goals, context and constraints. It asks follow-up questions to guide the search. With a verified, complete company account, compare approved profiles, relevant experience and missing information. You decide whom to consider.

Talk to BlooMIA
What does Workbloom cost?

There are four one-time packages, with prices excluding VAT and no automatic renewal. Token, assessment and support allowances vary; the matching standard stays the same. Professional fees are separate and agreed directly. Purchases are not available yet.

For Bloomers

Applying, professional autonomy and responsibilities.

Can I apply to Workbloom if I work full-time?

You can apply online. First check your employment contract, required permissions, conflicts of interest, competition restrictions, working hours and rest requirements. Seek qualified advice; a private profile does not remove these obligations.

How to apply
Can I work as a Bloomer while employed full-time?

It depends on your circumstances. Workbloom cannot authorise the work or provide legal advice.

Check the applicable employment law, collective agreements, your employment contract and the proposed work with an employment lawyer. Consider duties of loyalty, exclusivity, conflicts of interest, working hours and rest requirements. Workbloom does not replace legal advice.

Do I need to tell my employer?

It depends on your employment contract, company policies and the proposed work.

Before accepting an engagement, check whether you must notify your employer or obtain written authorisation. Ask your employer or an employment adviser. A private profile does not remove your legal or contractual responsibilities.

Do I need to register for VAT?

It depends on the nature and frequency of your work and your tax position.

Ask an accountant how to structure the engagement and which tax and social security obligations apply. Workbloom does not manage your accounts, arrange VAT registration or provide tax advice.

How much time do I need to commit?

You decide your availability and agree the commitment directly with the company.

Agree working hours, duration, availability and deliverables directly. Keep your availability accurate and up to date. Do not accept work that conflicts with your main employment or existing commitments.

Can I decline an opportunity after joining the network?

Yes. Joining the network or accepting a contact request does not commit you to a project.

Reply to a contact request within 2 working days: you can accept or decline it. If you decline, the company gets its token back. An engagement begins only when you and the company agree scope, fees and timing. Applying neither guarantees nor obliges you to accept a project.

What happens if a project does not go as planned?

Project disputes are between the company and the professional, under their contract and applicable law.

The Success Guarantee covers how well a profile meets the original confirmed requirements, with human review and a possible token recredit. It does not guarantee the outcome of the work or provide a cash refund.

How the guarantee works
Are there any fees or subscriptions for Bloomers?

Applying and joining the network do not require a professional subscription. Agree fees directly with the company. Professional, tax and social security obligations and costs remain your responsibility.

Contact our team
What is the benefit of becoming a Bloomer?

Connect your experience with relevant business needs while choosing your availability, fees and engagements. Projects, minimum fees and higher income are not guaranteed.
