Apply to the network of independent consultants and managers, known as Bloomers. The Workbloom team reviews your background; you choose which opportunities to consider.

Create your Bloomer profile

Verify your email and complete your profile online. Importing a CV or LinkedIn PDF is optional.

How to apply

  1. 1
    Create your account

    Enter your first name, surname, email and a strong password.

  2. 2
    Confirm your email

    Open the emailed link: in the same browser you continue without signing in again.

  3. 3
    Complete and submit your profile

    Prepare your experience, a project, skills and availability; you can import a CV or LinkedIn PDF and review the details.

The time needed depends on the details to review. You can save a draft and return later before submitting it to the team.

What you need to apply

  • Documented experience and projects.
  • Established specialist expertise.
  • Accurate information to share with the team.

How your application is reviewed

Complete your profile and speak with the team before a decision is made on your application.

A non-public profile

Once approved, your experience can be compared by companies with a verified, complete account. An unlock shows your name; your contact details only if you accept the request. Your profile is not listed in a public directory.

Before you apply

Can I apply to Workbloom if I work full-time?

You can apply online. First check your employment contract, required permissions, conflicts of interest, competition restrictions, working hours and rest requirements. Seek qualified advice; a private profile does not remove these obligations.

Do I need to register for VAT?

It depends on the nature and frequency of your work and your tax position. Check tax and social security obligations with an accountant. Workbloom does not provide tax advice or manage your accounts.

Are there any fees or subscriptions for Bloomers?

Applying and joining the network do not require a professional subscription. Agree fees directly with the company. Professional, tax and social security obligations and costs remain your responsibility.

All questions for Bloomers

Information on employment, contracts and tax is not professional advice. Before accepting assignments, check your circumstances with your employer and a qualified adviser.
