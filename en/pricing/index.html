What tokens and assessments cover

Identity unlock

One token unlocks a professional’s name and full profile. Contact details follow once they accept your request. Contact does not guarantee an engagement.

Detailed assessment

The assessment compares a profile against the brief and highlights gaps. One use is deducted only when the report is complete. It does not certify claims or guarantee outcomes.

Rules and limits

Token rules

A token is used only when the identity unlock succeeds. Search and brief explanations of results use no tokens. If an error prevents the unlock, your balance stays unchanged.

A contact request starts a conversation. It does not mean a project has been accepted, guarantee availability or establish an agreement on fees.

Assessment limits

The assessment compares your brief with the expertise and years of experience stated in the profile. It shows fit, gaps and missing information before unlock.

One assessment is deducted only after a complete report. Errors or incomplete results do not use assessments. Authorised company users can view report history on the platform.

Success guarantee

The Success guarantee covers the profile’s suitability against the original confirmed requirements. It does not guarantee project results. It may provide a token re-credit, not a cash refund.

Guarantee conditions

The period shown in your package begins on the first communication date, not at unlock. Submit your claim within that period and explain which original requirement was not met. The team reviews each claim. The response target is 5 working days; this may be extended if more information is needed.

If the Bloomer declines the contact request or does not accept it within 2 working days, the token used is automatically re-credited. An approved claim may also lead to a token re-credit. Each match is limited to one re-credit in total, whether for a decline, non-acceptance or a claim.

The guarantee does not provide cash refunds or limit your statutory rights. Changing packages does not extend the guarantee period for previous matches.

Package questions

Speak with our team
Is this a recurring subscription?

No. Each package is a one-time purchase, with no recurring fee or automatic renewal.

Does the matching standard change by package?

No. The matching standard is the same across all packages. Token and assessment allowances and support differ, not profile quality or matching priority.

Can I add extra tokens?

Extra tokens cost €49 + VAT each. Welcome, Starter and Growth allow up to 3 extra tokens per package cycle; Premium has no limit. The limit does not affect tokens you already hold.

What happens if I change packages?

Each new package is purchased at full price, without a proportional discount for the previous package. It activates only when payment is confirmed; pending or failed payments do not add tokens or assessments. Unused tokens and assessments carry over and are added to the new allowances. Changing packages does not include free extra tokens or extend the guarantee period for a previous match.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Displayed prices exclude VAT. Applicable tax is calculated and added at checkout from your billing details and tax jurisdiction, with reverse charge where applicable.

Do tokens and assessments expire?

Tokens and assessments do not expire during normal account use. Unused allowances carry over and are added to later purchases. Indefinite retention and automatic cash refunds are not guaranteed.

Can I view profiles or buy a package now?

You can chat with BlooMIA for free. With a verified, complete company account, you can compare profiles: an unlock shows the name and full profile, and contact details follow once the Bloomer accepts. Purchases are not available yet. Contact info@workbloom.io with package enquiries.
